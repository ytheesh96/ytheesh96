# Hi, I am Vaitheesh Jeyapalan

I am a PhD chemist (University of Miami) building scientific software and AI tools for chemistry. My doctoral research was in aqueous photochemistry, combining spectroscopy and analytical method development with quantum chemistry and molecular dynamics.

## Tools I built

- [DOSY-Calculator](https://github.com/ytheesh96/DOSY-Calculator) — browser-based NMR diffusion analysis with species assignment and error propagation ([live demo](https://ytheesh96.github.io/DOSY-Calculator/))
- [T1-Calculator](https://github.com/ytheesh96/T1-Calculator) — T1 relaxation time analysis from NMR data with interactive peak editing
- [Titration-calculator](https://github.com/ytheesh96/Titration-calculator) — titration planning, calculation, and simulation ([live demo](https://ytheesh96.github.io/Titration-calculator/))
- [MD_Simulator](https://github.com/ytheesh96/MD_Simulator) — molecular dynamics visualization of radial distribution functions ([live demo](https://ytheesh96.github.io/MD_Simulator/))
- [research-data-automation-tools](https://github.com/ytheesh96/research-data-automation-tools) — hub for chemistry calculators, NMR/DOSY analysis, and an AI-assisted photodimerization scout
- [photodimerization-scout](https://github.com/ytheesh96/photodimerization-scout) — plan the next irradiation experiment from a simple experiment readout

## Research

Four peer-reviewed publications spanning photodimerization of olefins in water, visible-light supramolecular photocatalysis, and photochromic transition-metal complexes.
